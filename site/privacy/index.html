Privacy notice

Updated 10 October 2026

This notice describes the independent Streamlab Platform website during its migration from Wix.

Visiting this website

The website serves its pages, images, videos and fonts from the same host. This version does not include advertising trackers, third-party analytics or embedded social-media feeds. Sharing buttons open the service you choose only when you activate them.

Hosting

When this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles requests for its pages and files and may process technical information such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for the host’s practices.

Contact and newsletter forms

The contact and newsletter submission services are being migrated. Until a service is connected, the forms explicitly explain that your information has not been sent. Typed entries remain in the current page and are not stored as a mailing list in your browser. This notice must be updated when submission services are connected.

Motion preferences

The website reads your device’s reduced-motion setting and offers a motion control. Your selection applies to the current page; this version does not store that choice in cookies.

Existing Wix information

Previous enquiries, newsletter subscriptions, member records and other information collected on the Wix website remain with the existing Wix service. They are not copied into this static website.

Contact Streamlab

Contact details are shown in the footer of the Streamlab homepage. When using email, your email service and the recipient’s email service handle the message.
